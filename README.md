# eCommerce Conversion & A/B Testing Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the eCommerce customer journey to identify checkout friction, evaluate an A/B test, and develop data-driven recommendations to improve conversion and revenue.

Using a simulated dataset for a fictional online retailer, Stride Commerce, the analysis explores customer behavior, purchase funnel performance, and the impact of a redesigned checkout experience.

**Project Type:** Business Analytics & eCommerce Optimization

**Industry:** Retail / eCommerce

**Data:** Simulated data created for portfolio purposes

## Business Problem

Stride Commerce is experiencing customer drop-off during the online purchasing process. Leadership wants to understand where customers abandon their carts and whether a redesigned checkout experience can improve conversion.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze the customer journey from product discovery to purchase.
- Identify conversion funnel drop-off points.
- Evaluate an A/B test comparing the existing checkout with a redesigned experience.
- Assess statistical significance and potential business impact.
- Develop actionable recommendations for eCommerce leadership.

## Tools & Skills

- **eCommerce Analytics:** Conversion rate, cart abandonment, and revenue performance

## Key Performance Indicators

- **Conversion Rate:** Percentage of visitors who complete a purchase.
- **Add-to-Cart Rate:** Percentage of visitors who add a product to their cart.
- **Checkout Completion Rate:** Percentage of checkout starters who complete a purchase.
- **Cart Abandonment Rate:** Percentage of shopping carts that do not result in a purchase.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Average revenue generated per order.
- **Revenue per Visitor (RPV):** Total revenue divided by the number of visitors.
